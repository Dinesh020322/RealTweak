 A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Scan. Design. Step inside.

  1. Start with your space. Scan a room in the RealTweak AR app and enter its code, create a room from a template, draw an outline, or import a room file.
  2. Adjust the shell. Drag corner handles, double-click walls to add corners, type exact wall lengths and ceiling height. Add doors, windows and interior walls under Build.
  3. Add floors. Use ＋ Floor or plan above the canvas. Floors stack on the building; place a staircase on the lower floor and it reaches the floor above, with a glowing route in 3D. Separate plans (garage, annex) sit beside it. All share one room code.
  4. Furnish it. Choose from 80+ models — furniture, kitchens, bathrooms, lighting and staircases. Click to place, drag to move, use the rotate handle, and set exact sizes in the details panel.
  5. Send it to the phone. Save to cloud (or turn on Live sync). The AR app updates instantly; the details panel shows which phones applied your latest version. Edits made on the phone at the same time are merged, not lost.

Ctrl/Cmd Z: undo · Ctrl/Cmd Shift Z: redo · Ctrl/Cmd S: save · Ctrl/Cmd D: duplicate · Delete: remove · Escape: select · Arrow keys: move · R: rotate 15°
